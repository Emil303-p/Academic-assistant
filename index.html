Academic Assistant

Project Overview

Academic Assistant is an AI-powered productivity assistant designed to help students and professionals manage their assignments, schedules, emails, research and daily tasks.

Key Features

  • Assignment and deadline reminders
  • Email summarization
  • Research assistance
  • Flashcard generation
  • Study quiz generation
  • Task and calendar management

Supported SDGs

  • SDG 4 - Quality Education
  • SDG 9 - Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure
  • SDG 10 - Reduced Inequalities
